Calculating Working Hours

Script for calculating claim hours

import pandas as pd
import re

claim_info = """
16 May 2024	Project Name	3 hrs		Approved
17 May 2024	Project Name	2 hrs		Approved
20 May 2024	Project Name	2 hrs 30 mins		Approved
21 May 2024	Project Name	4 hrs 30 mins		Approved
22 May 2024	Project Name	1 hr 30 mins		Approved
27 May 2024	Project Name	2 hrs 30 mins		Approved
28 May 2024	Project Name	4 hrs 30 mins		Approved
31 May 2024	Project Name	2 hrs 30 mins		Approved
04 Jun 2024	Project Name	4 hrs		Submitted
05 Jun 2024	Project Name	4 hrs		Submitted
07 Jun 2024	Project Name	2 hrs		Submitted
04 Jun 2024	Project Name	4 hrs		Approved
05 Jun 2024	Project Name	4 hrs		Approved
"""

df_all_info = pd.DataFrame(
    [list(filter(lambda x: len(x) > 0, i.split("\t"))) for i in filter(lambda x: len(x) > 0, claim_info.split("\n"))]
)
df_all_info.columns = ["Date", "Role", "Hours", "Status"]

# Regular expression to capture hours and minutes
pattern = r'(\d+)\s+hrs(?:\s+(\d+)\s+mins)?'
total= 0
for i in df_all_info[df_all_info["Status"] == "Approved"]["Hours"]:
    matches = re.findall(pattern, i)
    if len(matches) > 0:
        hour, minute = matches[0]
        total += int(f"0{hour}") + int(f"0{minute}")/60
print("Total Claim Hours:", total)

Access Frequency - Intervention

Question**: Interventions that inflence Access Frequency

Key

  • Course Requirements and Assignments: The nature and frequency of assignments, quizzes, and exams can drive students to access course materials more frequently. Deadlines and project milestones also play a role.
  • Engaging Content: Courses that include interactive elements such as videos, discussions, and hands-on activities often see higher engagement. Gamification elements, like quizzes and leaderboards, can also increase access frequency.
  • Instructor Engagement: Active participation and feedback from instructors can encourage students to visit course platforms more often. Regular announcements, office hours, and Q&A sessions can keep students engaged.
  • Peer Interaction: Discussion forums, group projects, and peer feedback opportunities can motivate students to access educational resources more frequently to engage with their classmates.
  • Learning Tools and Resources: Availability of supplementary materials like readings, videos, tutorials, and external resources can encourage students to explore and revisit the content.
  • Accessibility and Convenience: The ease of access to the platform or resources, including mobile-friendly interfaces, offline access, and intuitive navigation, can significantly impact how often students engage with the material.
  • Personal Motivation and Goals: Students' individual motivation, such as interest in the subject, career aspirations, and personal goals, can drive more frequent engagement with educational resources.
  • Feedback and Assessment: Regular feedback, assessments, and the opportunity to track progress can motivate students to engage more frequently.
