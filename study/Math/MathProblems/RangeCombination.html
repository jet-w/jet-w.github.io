How to Get Combination Range

Haiyue10/22/24About 2 minmath

Background

There are three variables, name target,candidate and others,

  • The range of target is [a,b].
  • The range of candidate is [c,d].
  • The range of others is [e,f].

There is a relationshp: target=candidate+others
Now give the range of target [x1,x2], to find the available combination for candidate and others.

Solution

  • Step 1: Find the value of candidate v∈[c,d].

  • Step 2: [e+v,f+v]∩[x1,x2]≠\empty

  • Step 3: max(e+v,x1)≤min(f+v,x2)

  • Step 4:
    {x1≤v+fif x1≥e+v and x2≥f+ve+v≤v+fif x1≤e+v and x2≥f+vx1≤x2if x1≥e+v and x2≤f+ve+v≤x2if x1≤e+v and x2≤f+v
    ⇓
    {v≥x1−fif x1≥e+v and x2≥f+ve≤fif x1≤e+v and x2≥f+vx1≤x2if x1≥e+v and x2≤f+vv≤x2−eif x1≤e+v and x2≤f+v
    ⇓

  • Range 1:
    {v≥x1−fv≤x1−ev≤x2−fv≥cv≤d ⇒ v∈[max(x1−f,c),min(x1−e,x2−f,d)]

  • Range 2:
    {v≥x1−ev≤x2−fv≥cv≤d ⇒ v∈[max(x1−e,c),min(x2−f,d)]

  • Range 3:
    {v≥x2−fv≤x1−ev≥cv≤d ⇒ v∈[max(x2−f,c),min(x1−e,d)]

  • Range 4:
    {v≤x2−ev≥x1−ev≥x2−fv≥cv≤d ⇒ v∈[max(x1−e,x2−f,c),min(x2−e,d)]

  • Step 5:

    1. select a value v in available ranges for candidate,
    2. update date target to [x1-v, x2-v]
    3. select one element from others as candidate and remove it from others
    • if others is not empty,
      4. update others as the sum of others elements (except new candidateelement)
      5. Go to Step 1.
    • else go to Step 6.

  • Step 6:
    select a random value from the intersection of target and candidate.
